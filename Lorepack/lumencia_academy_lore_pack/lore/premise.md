# Setting Premise

Lumencia Academy is a prestigious imperial institution in a medieval fantasy world where martial cultivation, academic spellcraft and divine power coexist. The academy is the main stage, but it also acts as a small-scale reflection of the wider empire: noble privilege, commoner ambition, imperial succession politics, religious diplomacy, commercial influence and hidden demonic threats all intersect there.

The experience is best understood as an **open-ended academy fantasy role-playing simulation**, rather than a linear novel with one universally fixed sequence of events. The protagonist is player-controlled. The world, non-player characters (NPCs), faculty and consequences are run by the narrative system. School life—classes, examinations, rivalries, clubs, reputation and relationships—can coexist with dangerous expeditions, court intrigue and cult activity.

## Core themes

- Personal growth earned through training, study, mistakes and consequences.
- Noble lineage versus individual merit.
- Martial skill, scholarly magic and faith-based power as distinct paths.
- Public reputation versus private motives.
- Rivalry, mentorship, alliance-building and social maneuvering.
- Romance, built slowly and earned through interaction.
- The tension between ordinary school life and the empire's larger dangers.

## Structural premise

- The Lumencia Empire is the dominant political power across central and western Aethermere.
- Asterion is its capital and the location of Lumencia Academy.
- The academy is officially neutral and under imperial jurisdiction, but major factions compete for influence there.
- The Holy Kingdom of Dominus follows the deity Lily and has its own tradition of Divinity.
- The Demon God Cult seeks the return of Delphirem and uses Miasma, a power distinct from ordinary mana.
- The eastern Abyss Zone is a major source of demonic incursions and Miasma.

## Canon and freedom

Everything written in this pack is canon for every story. Where a detail is deliberately left open (the content of a prophecy, what someone lost, who the hidden Acolytes are), the narrator decides it in each story and then keeps it consistent.
