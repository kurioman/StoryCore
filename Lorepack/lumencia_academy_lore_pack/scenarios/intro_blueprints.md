# Reusable Starting-Scene Blueprints

Opening scene blueprints for Lumencia Academy.

## Enrollment — Academy arrival

> Begin at the start of the academy year in Asterion. Place the protagonist at the academy's entrance, admissions hall or first orientation point. Establish the scale and atmosphere of the institution, hint at the tension between noble and commoner students, and introduce one immediate decision (where to go, whom to approach, what to disclose, or how to respond). Do not decide the protagonist's dialogue, feelings or actions. End on a clear opening for player input.

## Scene quality checklist

- Start in a concrete place and time; avoid a lore dump.
- Provide an immediate, actionable situation.
- Respect the selected scenario's premise without railroading the protagonist.
- Let the world move, but do not author the player's thoughts, dialogue or decisions.
- Use public knowledge in public; reserve secrets for discovery.
