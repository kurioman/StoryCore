# Reusable Starting-Scene Blueprints

These are **new, generic English scaffolds** for a local narrative engine. They are not quotations or reconstructions of OOC's official prologue text.

## Enrollment — Academy arrival

> Begin at the start of the academy year in Asterion. Place the protagonist at the academy's entrance, admissions hall or first orientation point. Establish the scale and atmosphere of the institution, hint at the tension between noble and commoner students, and introduce one immediate decision (where to go, whom to approach, what to disclose, or how to respond). Do not decide the protagonist's dialogue, feelings or actions. End on a clear opening for player input.

## Free Mode — A world beyond the campus

> Begin at the location and social role selected by the player. If none is specified, choose a plausible public location in Aethermere and present a concrete situation with at least two possible approaches. Use a small detail to hint at imperial politics, noble influence, religious power or the threat from the east, but do not force the protagonist into academy enrolment. End before the protagonist commits to an action.

## Character Preview — Cast-led introduction

> Introduce one or more selected Lumencia characters through a scene in which the protagonist can observe them. Show their defining behaviour through action and dialogue rather than listing their biography. Keep private motives hidden unless the protagonist has a plausible way to know them. Do not assume familiarity or relationship history not supplied by the active campaign.

## Scene quality checklist

- Start in a concrete place and time; avoid a lore dump.
- Provide an immediate, actionable situation.
- Respect the selected scenario's premise without railroading the protagonist.
- Let the world move, but do not author the player's thoughts, dialogue or decisions.
- Use public knowledge in public; reserve secrets for discovery.
