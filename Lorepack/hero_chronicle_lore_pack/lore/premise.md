# Setting Premise: Arche Continent

The Arche Continent is a vast world where magic and aura coexist in a perpetual struggle for power. It is a land of diverse races (Humans, Elves, Beastkin, Dwarves, Dragonkin) and a rigid social structure.

## Worldview Overview
A classic medieval fantasy world where humans, other races, and monsters wage endless struggles for power.

## Races and Homelands
- **Humans**: everywhere on the continent; main capital Grandel (Central Empire, Aether).
- **Elves**: Lumirien (northern forest, Council of Elders). Hostile to the Dwarves.
- **Dwarves**: Dorvalkar (underground mountains of the northwest).
- **Beastkin**: Moonlight Forest (southern forest, chieftain system).
- **Dragonkin**: Wyvern Pavilion (caldera of the Vendetta Mountains).
- **Demons**: no capital or homeland zone; all over the Demon Realm.

## Class System
- **Title System**: 6 ranks of Grand Duke, Duke, Marquis, Count, Viscount, and Baron.
- **Noble Class Divisions**: Knight (military), Lord (governance), Chancellor (administration).
- **Commoner Class Divisions**: Adventurer, Soldier, Merchant, Artisan, Farmer, Slave.

## Currency System
- 1G (Gold) = 100S (Silver)
- 1S (Silver) = 100C (Copper)
- Average monthly living cost for a commoner: 5S.

## System of Power
- **Martial Realm (武道境)**: Focuses on Aura (Body Enhancement, Sword Energy, Sword Force). 
  - *Ranks (fighters, swordsmen...)*: Beginner (Low / Mid / High) < Intermediate (Low / Mid / High) < Expert (Low / Mid / High) < Master-Initiate < Master-Proficient < Master-Pinnacle.
- **Magic Realm (魔道境)**: Focuses on Mana.
  - *Circles*: 1st to 9th Circle.
- **Sword Saint**: a title held by the strongest swordsman alive.
  - *Archmage Title*: Granted for 7th Circle and above.
- **Holy Power**: Manifested through the Church of Lily, serving the Heavenly God Lily.
- **Miasma**: A power distinct from ordinary mana, used by the Demon God Cult.

## Core Themes
- Personal growth through training and consequences.
- Noble lineage vs. individual merit.
- The tension between ordinary life and the empire's larger dangers.
- Public reputation vs. private motives.
