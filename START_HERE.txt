STORYCORE v0.5 (Windows)
Quick start. Takes about a minute to read.

You do not need to install Python, Node.js or anything else.

1. EXTRACT
   Right-click the ZIP and choose "Extract All...". Pick a normal folder
   such as Documents or Desktop. Do not run anything from inside the ZIP
   window, and avoid Program Files. Extraction can take a minute or two.

2. START
   Open the StoryCore folder and double-click:

       Start StoryCore.bat

   This beta is not code-signed, so Windows may show "Windows protected
   your PC". Choose "More info", then "Run anyway".

   A black window opens, then your browser opens the app. Keep the black
   window open while you play. Close it when you are done.

3. CHOOSE AN AI
   In the app, open Settings, then Providers & models. Follow the short
   guide on that page. Three common choices:

   - LM Studio (local models, free to run): in LM Studio, load a model and
     start its local server (Developer tab). In StoryCore, under All
     providers, press Add provider, choose "LM Studio (local)", then add
     the loaded model and select it.
   - Ollama (local models, free to run): you install Ollama and a model
     yourself. Results depend on your PC.
   - OpenRouter (hosted models): you need your own account and API key.
     Usage is billed by OpenRouter, to you. Set a spending limit on the key.

   You can try the app first with the built-in "Mock narrator". It writes
   clearly labelled demo text only, not a real story.

4. PLAY
   Choose New story and go through the five short steps. On the second
   step, choose a World (for example "Hero's Chronicle" or "Lumencia
   Academy") and one of its openings, or you get a story with no lore.
   At the end, choose "Generate the opening scene", then write what your
   character does. The ? Help button in the sidebar gives a short tour.

Character portraits and relationship tracking are on by default, so they
work as soon as a model is connected. Portraits appear as soon as a reply
ends. Three suggested replies can be turned on in Settings, Memory capture.
On a paid model the scene reading uses one extra request per reply.

Your stories stay on this PC, in the "data" folder inside the StoryCore folder.
If you delete that folder, you delete your stories. To back up, use
Settings, Data & backups, Back up now.

Something wrong? See "Troubleshooting" in README.md.
Bug reports go to the person who shared StoryCore with you.

Developers: the DEVONLY folder explains how everything works.
